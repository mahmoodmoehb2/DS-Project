How has the frequency, intensity (peak and average temperature), and duration of
heatwaves in major German cities (>100,000 inhabitants) changed between 1980 and today?

RQ1

We took a look at 57 German cities with more than 150,000 inhabitants and analyzed 45 years of daily maximum temperatures for each one (1980–2025). For every single day, we check two things: was it unusually hot for that specific city (compared to the years 1961–1990), and was it hot in absolute terms (above 28°C)? Only if at least 3 days in a row meet both conditions does it count as a heatwave.

Which cities are we looking at?

57 German cities with more than 150,000 inhabitants, loaded via a Wikidata query. Instead of just a table: a simple map where dot size shows population. Berlin, Hamburg, and Munich stand out at a glance as the three largest dots.
