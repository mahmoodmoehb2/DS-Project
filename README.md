# DS-Project 

## Climate in Europe

### Project Overview 
This Data Science project investigates long-term changes in heatwaves
and UV radiation across Germany and Europe.

The analysis focuses on temporal trends as well as differences between
urban and rural areas. We examine how the frequency, intensity and
duration of heatwaves have changed and investigate regional trends in
UV exposure.

### Research Questions
1. How have the frequency, intensity (peak and average temperature), and duration of heatwaves in major German cities (≥100,000 inhabitants) changed between 1980 and today?
2. How do heatwave frequency, intensity (peak and average temperature), and duration differ across City sizes, from large cities to rural municipalities, within the same region in Germany?
3. How have the frequency, intensity (peak and average temperature), and duration of heatwaves in major european cities (≥500,000 inhabitants) changed between 1980 and today?
4. In the European country most strongly affected by rising heatwave trends, does this effect concentrate in large cities, or is it equally present in smaller cities and rural municipalities?
5. How has the UV Index across Europe changed over the past 20 years, and are there significant temporal and regional trends?
6. What are the public health implications of rising UV Index trends in highly affected European regions, particularly regarding sunburn risk?

### Methodology
The project follows a typical Data Science workflow:

1. Data collection
2. Data cleaning and preprocessing
3. Geographic mapping of coordinates
4. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
5. Definition and detection of heatwaves
6. Temporal trend analysis
7. Urban vs. rural comparison
8. Visualization
9. Statistical analysis
10. Interpretation of results
